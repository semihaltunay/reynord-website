01 How it works

From card tap to approved hours.

The F300 is the clock on the wall at each customer site; CleanSys is the office behind it. Every tap becomes a verified, tamper-evident record that the right people can act on.

  1. Set up

    Customers, sites, employees and their RFID cards are managed in CleanSys.

  2. Tap

    The cleaner taps a MIFARE card on the F300 at the site to clock in or out.

  3. Transmit

    The F300 sends the clock event over an encrypted connection, numbered and hash-chained to the previous event.

  4. Verify

    CleanSys checks the chain and sequence, flags clock drift, identifies the employee by card UID and stores the event in an append-only log.

  5. Approve

    Accountants review flagged work sessions, with night and Sunday time shown separately, and approve them.

04 German compliance

Built around German rules.

CleanSys is purpose-built for the legal and day-to-day realities of the German cleaning sector, not adapted from a generic HR tool.

  • GoBD

    Tamper-evident records

    Clock events are append-only, enforced in the database itself. Corrections are stored as new entries that reference the original, so the full history stays auditable.

  • § 17 MiLoG · § 19 AEntG

    Working-time recording

    Every clock-in and clock-out is stored with both the terminal's time and the server's time of receipt.

  • DSGVO

    Separated company data

    Each company's data lives in its own isolated database schema, so a deletion request can remove a company's data completely.

  • § 3b EStG

    Premium time made visible

    Time approval shows night and Sunday time separately from regular hours.

CleanSys records and prepares working time for payroll (pre-payroll). Taxes, social-security contributions and net pay are calculated in your payroll system.

05 F300 + CleanSys

Records that hold up to an audit.

The terminal and the platform are designed together, so a clock event cannot be quietly changed, dropped or invented between the wall and the payroll office.

  • Gap-free numbering

    Every terminal numbers its events in sequence; a missing or repeated number is detected.

  • Hash chain

    Each event contains the SHA-256 hash of the previous one, so editing or deleting any event breaks the chain.

  • Duplicate-safe

    A batch that is sent twice is recognised, and no event is ever stored twice.

  • Two clocks

    Terminal time and server time are both kept. Drift beyond a configurable limit is flagged or rejected.

  • Encrypted transport

    The F300 talks to CleanSys over HTTPS with TLS 1.3.
